# Build

Read `task.md`, `PROJECT_CONTEXT.md`, and the current state. Trace the affected flow and callers. Make the smallest change within the permitted scope that can satisfy a criterion. Record changed paths and the reason in the state. Do not change unrelated work. Follow the project's software-change or Gauntlet workflow where applicable.
